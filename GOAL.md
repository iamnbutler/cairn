# Goal: M4, Sky: reading Bluesky

The current milestone is [M4](docs/goals/m4.md): Sky, the reading half of a Bluesky client, in
its own repository and built only from packages. It brings scrolling (a scroll box and a virtual
list), secrets in the Keychain and secure fields, and `cairn_atproto`.

Finished milestones are in [docs/goals/](docs/goals/): [M1](docs/goals/m1.md) TodoMVC,
[M2](docs/goals/m2.md) Yap and [M3](docs/goals/m3.md) Pairs. What comes after M4 is in
[docs/roadmap.md](docs/roadmap.md).

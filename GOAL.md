# Goal: M5, Bluesky: act

M4 is done: [Sky](docs/goals/m4.md) reads Bluesky, in its own repository and built only from
packages, with scrolling (a scroll box and a virtual list), secrets in the Keychain, secure
fields and `cairn_atproto` in place. The next milestone is M5, the acting half of the client:
like, repost, follow and mute shown at once and rolled back on failure, with the count proved to
match the server's after any order of replies, and notifications. Its goal document isn't
written yet; [docs/roadmap.md](docs/roadmap.md) has the outline.

Finished milestones are in [docs/goals/](docs/goals/): [M1](docs/goals/m1.md) TodoMVC,
[M2](docs/goals/m2.md) Yap, [M3](docs/goals/m3.md) Pairs and [M4](docs/goals/m4.md) Sky.

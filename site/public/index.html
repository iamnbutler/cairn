Cairn

Research into user interfaces where everything a person can do is a claim with a durable id, and every claim carries machine-checked evidence.

The idea

A cairn is a stack of stones that marks a trail: placed by hand, built to last, and trusted by whoever comes next. Here the trails are a product's flows, and the stones are claims about them: “pressing ^12 clears the completed items”, “^12's button is visible and on top whenever there's something to clear”. Each claim is backed by a Lean proof, a model check or a conformance run against the real app.

To make those claims mean something, the code they talk about has to be the code that runs. So the app's state, view, layout, hit testing, focus and text editing are all pure Lean, and the theorems are about exactly those functions.

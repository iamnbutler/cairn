Columns

Proved. Every theorem the claim names (each move handler's sound; a flow's live and reaches; an invariant's init, step and reachable) is in lake exe audit --json and depends only on propext, Classical.choice and Quot.sound. make check also replays every declaration through the kernel.

Assumed. The claim is an assume: recorded, never proved. Theorems that rely on it take it as a hypothesis.

Conformance. Not run: workstream J's driver will write build/conformance/<App>.json. Until then this column is a placeholder.

Seen. A headless capture (the app's own step and frame, rendered offscreen by CairnRender) shows the control, pinned from its accessibility rect; for a flow, every witness step was performed on its control (a click at its centre; text and keys to the focused field).

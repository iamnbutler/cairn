Cairn.Frame.Build

lean/Cairn/Frame/Build.lean

Frame.ofLaid: a laid-out tree becomes what crosses the boundary: draw commands in paint order, the accessibility tree, and the focused field's text-input state.

  • Paint order (back to front, depth first). A box paints its background and border (one rect, only when it has either), then its children, wrapped in pushClip/popClip of its rect when it clips. Text paints one text at its baseline origin. A field paints its background and border, then, clipped to its rect, its selection rects, its text (or its placeholder at half alpha when the text is empty), and its caret when focused and the selection is empty.
  • Accessibility. One AxNode per control (boxes with a control, fields) and per text, in paint order, each with its visible rect (rect ∩ clip). A node's parent is the nearest enclosing control. A field's value is its text and its role is textField.
  • Text input describes the first field whose control has focus.

Focus comes from the runtime (UI.focus), the one source of truth; FieldView.focused is for the view's own styling and is not read here. Laid.text's baseline is absolute (window y). Laid.controls is layout's (Layout/Facts.lean). controls_ofLaid_layout, which composes controls_ofLaid with layout, is in Frame/Layout.lean.

Theorems: controls_ofLaid (the accessibility tree's controls are exactly the laid tree's, in order, which is how presence reaches the accessibility mirror), mem_ax_ofLaid, ax_refs_nodup (distinct refs in the tree give distinct refs in the mirror), parent_lt_ofLaid (every parent index points at an earlier node) and balanced_ofLaid (clips are balanced).

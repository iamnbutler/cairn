Cairn.Runtime.World

lean/Cairn/Runtime/World.lean

The runtime's view of the outside world (docs/effects.md): the clock, the app's timers, its pending requests and its active subscriptions. Pure tables and the functions that update them; Cairn.Runtime.Step decides when they run, and Cairn.Runtime.WorldProofs says what they guarantee.

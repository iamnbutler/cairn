Cairn by Example

Counter

A count, an Increment button, and a Reset button that is there only when there is something to reset.

Counter is the smallest complete Cairn app. Every part of the stack is in it: claims, a view, proofs that reach the screen, runtime tests, a Swift host, a self test and a map. Your first app walks through it in order; this page is the reference.

IdKindWhat
^1moveIncrement, always enabled
^2moveReset, enabled only when the count is above zero
^3placethe window
^4, ^5flowcount to three; count up, then reset
^6retiredan invariant the runtime made pointless
^7assumethe host reports clicks where they happen

Its map: /map/counter/.

The model and its claims

The view

What is proved

  • Each move does what its claim says (sound), and each flow's path is live and reaches its goal.
  • A press on anything that isn't ^1 or ^2 changes nothing.
  • Reset's control is in the view, in the frame's accessibility tree, and in every frame the running app shows, exactly when the count is above zero.
  • In any window at least 480 × 256, with any text measurements, a click at the centre of every control hits that control.

TodoMVC

The full TodoMVC spec as a native Mac app, with every behaviour a claim and the counts, filters and controls proved.

TodoMVC was Cairn's first milestone: a small app with a complete public spec, real text fields and enough edge cases to need every layer. Every one of the spec's fifty requirements maps to a claim or to code; each claim's does names the requirements it covers.

What is proved

  • Every move's post, for every state where it is enabled.
  • In every state the running app reaches, whatever events arrive: the counter equals the active todos, ids are distinct, and the rows are the filtered todos in order.
  • Every move's control is in the view, the frame's accessibility tree and on screen exactly when the move is enabled.
  • In any window at least 389 points wide and tall enough for the list, with any text measurements, a click at the centre of every control hits it. There is no scrolling yet, so rows below the window are outside what this says.

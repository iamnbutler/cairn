# Goal: M4, Sky: reading Bluesky

Cairn is research into building user interfaces where every thing a person can do is a
**claim** with a durable id, and every claim carries machine-checked evidence. M1 proved the loop
on TodoMVC, M2 on Yap and M3 on Pairs, which brought images, JSON and requests in flight. M4 to M7
build a Bluesky client ([roadmap](../roadmap.md)). M4 is the reading half: sign in, keep the
session, and read the home feed, however long it gets.

Sky signs in with a handle and an app password, keeps the session in the Keychain, and shows the
Following feed. Scrolling to the end loads the next page; refresh puts new posts on top without
moving what you're reading. Nothing in it likes, posts or follows yet (M5, M6).

## The demo

Sky lives in its own repository, `iamnbutler/sky` (package `sky`, claims `sky^N`), and is built
only through the package path: `cairn new sky --bin`, `cairn add cairn`, `cairn add cairn_json`,
`cairn add cairn_atproto`, `cairn bundle --sign`. Nate signs in with an app password and reads
his timeline with it for a session. Every path also runs against a fake PDS on `127.0.0.1`, so
nothing needs the real network or a real account to be tested.

## Done means

1. **The app.** Sign in (handle or email, app password, the service), errors in words; the
   session resumes at launch and Sign Out forgets it; the home feed with avatar, name, handle,
   relative time, wrapped text, up to four image thumbnails, repost and reply lines, read-only
   counts, and one line for other embeds; load more at the end; refresh with ⌘R and a button;
   a row opens the post on bsky.app. ([#210](https://github.com/iamnbutler/cairn/issues/210),
   [#211](https://github.com/iamnbutler/cairn/issues/211))
2. **Scrolling in Cairn.** The framework, not the app, gains:
   - a **scroll box** in layout: content longer than the box, drawn shifted by an offset and
     clipped, with pressability restated over each control's visible part
     ([#202](https://github.com/iamnbutler/cairn/issues/202));
   - **offsets owned by the runtime**, as focus and hover are: the wheel, trackpad and page keys
     move them, the runtime clamps them, and a scroll never goes through the app's `update`. An
     app hears that something came on screen through `Action.appear`, a move like any other
     ([#203](https://github.com/iamnbutler/cairn/issues/203));
   - a **virtual list** (`Cairn.UI.VList`, like `Grid`): the view names every row, but only rows
     that meet the viewport are built and laid. Heights are never measured off screen: a row's
     height is an estimate until it is laid, then the runtime feeds the real one back, and an
     anchor keeps the first visible row still while heights and rows above it change
     ([#204](https://github.com/iamnbutler/cairn/issues/204)).
3. **Secrets in Cairn.**
   - **The Keychain, in the host.** A request's secret header can come from a Keychain item, and
     a reply's top-level JSON fields can be captured into Keychain items and blanked before Lean
     sees the body. The host knows nothing about Bluesky; Lean names items, never holds their
     values. The Keychain is an `assume` claim in the trusted base with a fuzz check, like the
     dotenv secret Yap uses ([#205](https://github.com/iamnbutler/cairn/issues/205)).
   - **Secure fields**: bullets, no copy or cut, no undo history, an `AXSecureTextField`, secure
     event input while focused, and `•••` in every dump and report
     ([#206](https://github.com/iamnbutler/cairn/issues/206)).
4. **`cairn_atproto`, a package.** XRPC requests and errors, decoders for the session calls and
   `getTimeline`, and the session machine: one refresh at a time on `ExpiredToken`, each call
   retried at most once, a failed refresh signs out. Lean only; published to the registry
   ([#207](https://github.com/iamnbutler/cairn/issues/207)).
5. **Testable without the network.** A fake PDS twice over: a Lean model that `cairn fuzz` uses
   for replies (a timeline growing at its head, cursors, expiring and rotating tokens, faults),
   and a local server with the same behaviour that the real app runs against. Decoders are
   checked against recorded *public* responses. No credentials, tokens or private timelines in
   any repository ([#208](https://github.com/iamnbutler/cairn/issues/208)).
6. **Claims and proofs** with M1's rules (no `sorry`, standard axioms only):
   - *Scrolling:* every offset stays within `[0, max 0 (content − viewport)]` after every step;
     the rows laid are exactly those meeting the viewport (plus overscan), so their number is
     bounded by the viewport, not the list; rows inserted or resized above the anchor leave it
     where it was on screen; a scroll changes no app state; an enabled control with a visible
     part is pressed at that part's centre.
   - *Paging and refresh* (`sky^N`): the feed is a contiguous run of the server's timeline in
     its order, with no duplicates and no gaps; a load-more reply for a cursor the feed no
     longer holds never changes it; the cursor is never lost (a feed that isn't at its end holds
     the cursor of its last page); a refresh racing a load-more ends as one of the two
     sequential orders would; at most one of each in flight, and every request is answered,
     cancelled or live.
   - *Session:* no Lean value holds a token (by construction, and fuzzed with planted markers
     over every frame, reply, dump and log); the password is in exactly one request, cleared
     from its field in the step that sends it, and in no dump or log; at most one refresh in
     flight; signed out means no request carries a secret.
   - Presence under virtualization: a keyed control on a row that isn't laid is enabled but not
     shown, and the fuzzer's presence check says so; menus and enablement as before.
7. **Fuzzed, measured, written down.** Random scrolls, resizes, load-mores, refreshes, sign-outs,
   new posts at the server's head, expiring tokens, and replies slow, failed, out of order and
   duplicated, checking every claim each step. Frame time per scroll step flat from 100 to
   100,000 rows and within 8 ms at 10,000; sign-in to first page and refresh to new posts
   against the fake; memory after 1,000 posts; in `bench/`
   ([#212](https://github.com/iamnbutler/cairn/issues/212)). Every new assumption in
   `docs/trusted.md` ([#213](https://github.com/iamnbutler/cairn/issues/213)).

## Where the secrets sit

The access and refresh tokens exist only in the host and the Keychain: captured from the
`createSession` and `refreshSession` replies, sent as `Authorization` headers named by item,
deleted by `forgetSecret`. Lean holds the DID, handle and service URL. The app password is typed
text, so it passes through Lean once, in a secure field, and goes out in the one `createSession`
request, marked `redact`; Lean drops it in the same step. The policy (when to refresh, what to
retry, when to sign out) is Lean's, in `cairn_atproto`; the host only stores, attaches and
captures.

## Not in M4

Likes, reposts, follows, mutes and notifications (M5); posting, replies, quotes and rich text,
including styled or clickable links and mentions (M6: post text is plain wrapped text in M4);
columns, several windows and live updates (M7); threads, profiles, custom feeds and search;
several accounts; OAuth (app passwords only); video and GIF playback; keeping the feed across
launches; other platforms.
